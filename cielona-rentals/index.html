✦ OUR ATELIER STORY ✦

The Cielona Heritage

✧

Rooted in the French word ciel, meaning sky or heaven, Cielona was conceived with a romantic philosophy: that the most memorable evenings are written in the stars.

We believe that luxury formalwear should be accessible, thoughtful, and sustainable. Instead of purchasing an expensive gown that will only be worn once, Cielona offers a curated edit of timeless silhouettes for your proms, debuts, gala nights, and black-tie celebrations.

Based in Cabuyao City, Laguna, each garment is maintained with meticulous care and preserved to look pristine for every client who steps into our studio.

✦ CLEAR ANSWERS ✦

Frequently Asked Questions

✧
How long is the standard rental period? +

Our standard rental period is 3 full days. Day 1 is pickup / arrival, Day 2 is your special event, and Day 3 is return day. Extended rental periods can be arranged upon inquiry.

How much is the security deposit and is it refundable? +

A standard ₱500 PHP security deposit is required per dress rental. This is 100% refundable upon the safe return of the dress in its original condition without tears or permanent stains.

Do I need to wash or dry clean the gown before returning? +

No! Please do not attempt to wash, steam, or dry clean the gown yourself. Delicate fabrics and boning require specialized couture care. We handle all professional cleaning after each rental.

Where is your studio located and can I schedule a fitting? +

Our fitting studio is located in Cabuyao City, Laguna. Fittings are strictly by appointment to ensure privacy and dedicated assistance. Message us on Instagram to schedule your slot.

How do I reserve a gown for my event date? +

Simply click any dress card to inspect the rate and click INQUIRE TO BOOK or Copy Details for DM. Send the details to our Instagram page (@cielona.rentals) with your event date and location.
